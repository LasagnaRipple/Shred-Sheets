### 2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

Shred Sheets requests only the minimum Android permissions necessary to perform its core musical instrument tuning and practice features:

#### A. Microphone Access (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`)
- **Purpose:** Required strictly to capture live audio signals from your acoustic or electric instrument to detect pitch, frequency (in Hertz), and cent deviation in real time.
- **Ephemeral On-Device Processing:** All audio processing is executed 100% locally on your device hardware using in-memory digital signal processing (DSP / autocorrelation / FFT).
- **No Recording or Transmission:** Audio streams are **never** recorded, never saved to internal or external device storage, and never transmitted over the internet or to any third-party servers. Audio buffer frames are discarded immediately after calculating the pitch.

#### B. Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`)
- **Purpose:** Used exclusively to provide optional tactile haptic feedback when a string is tuned to pitch or when interacting with the metronome tempo beats.
- No data is accessed, logged, or gathered through vibration hardware.

### 3. Local Storage & App Settings

Shred Sheets saves user preferences (such as selected instrument, tuning preset, light/dark theme preference, accent color, and interface language) locally on your device using Android's private internal storage.
- These settings exist solely on your local device for your convenience so your configuration persists between sessions.
- This configuration data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you uninstall the App or clear app storage.
